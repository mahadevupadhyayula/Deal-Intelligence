## 2. What it achieves
A `lead_research` job researches a company and evaluates it against all active ICP segments. Every claim needs a URL plus a verbatim quote that code verifies in the fetched text; numbers and scores are computed in code; the LLM never sees or emits tenants, segment keys, scores or tiers. Unverifiable claims become `unknown`, wrong-company results are capped at `insufficient_data`, refine passes happen only when they could change the tier, and cost is bounded by caps on searches, LLM calls, sources and characters.
