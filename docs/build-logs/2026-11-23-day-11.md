## Findings (all fixed or recorded in the spec)
1. Kubernetes service links injected `WORKER_METRICS_PORT=tcp://...` and crashed the first migrate pod (name collision with our settings). Fixed with `enableServiceLinks: false` everywhere plus a test.
2. A worker-cron reaper would deadlock with scale-to-zero workers, so the reaper runs in the API.
3. `configure_logging` had silently undone `--no-access-log` (since Spec 009); fixed.
4. After a hard kill the orphaned queue entry keeps `di_queue_outstanding` above zero for about the job timeout.
5. With default-deny egress, the migrate and admin Jobs needed an explicit egress rule to Postgres.
6. The first full smoke failed only the in-cluster golden step because of my 120 s wait; the wait is now configurable.

## 2. What it achieves
The same image and environment contract now runs on Kubernetes with workers that scale from zero on the real backlog; a scale-down or deploy never fails a running job; a worker that dies hard no longer strands jobs or locks a tenant out; and the least-privilege secret model and pod hardening are enforced by the cluster itself.
