## 2. What it achieves
Tenants own versioned, multi-segment ICPs. Scores and tiers are computed by auditable code: numeric criteria (employee bands) are graded from the observed value, judged criteria use LLM verdicts, unknowns never count as bad fits, and thin evidence becomes `insufficient_data`.
